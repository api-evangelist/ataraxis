# Ataraxis AI

> Ataraxis AI is a precision-medicine company building clinical AI foundation models that make disease predictable, beginning with oncology. Its first product, Ataraxis Breast, is an AI-native, clinically validated prognostic and predictive test suite for breast cancer (Recurrence Risk, Chemotherapy Benefit, and Neoadjuvant Response). Ataraxis operates a clinician portal rather than a public developer API; there is no published OpenAPI, SDK, or developer documentation surface at this time.
